# Tutor protocol

Start with the guiding question and a short physical or computational model. Ask at most one
prediction before the baseline. Present one plot or transition at a time. After the learner observes
a change, connect it to the mechanism, units, and an earlier module. Correct misconceptions directly.
Do not tutor a scaffold as though it were implemented, and do not record completion until executable
checks pass and the learner gives a concise teach-back.
