## Changed invariants

- P09 defines one fixed startup order and one fixed shutdown order with explicit preconditions, observable
  postconditions, and fixed-size state traces.
- P08 contract conformance and input eligibility are separate startup facts; eligibility cannot be asserted
  for a nonconformant contract.
- Actuator enable at position 5 and power removal at position 6 are the inclusive valid ordering boundaries.
- The strict decision requires every evaluated startup/shutdown step plus running and safe-off snapshots.
  Snapshot-only reporting cannot change factual traces and exposes false approval when it discards order.
- Cancellation and timeout stop nominal evaluation after three proposed startup actions, isolate shutdown, and
  execute five compensating steps with separate guards, postconditions, hazard flags, and a rollback failure.
  Safe hold requires every rollback step plus the final facts; a stuck actuator or missing quiescence produces
  a distinct incomplete-rollback terminal. Cancellation wins the modeled tie. Unevaluated nominal shutdown
  state remains unavailable rather than aliasing the rollback terminal state.
- The model is stateless. A valid call after malformed input, invalid order, P08 rejection, shutdown fault,
  cancellation, timeout, or broken reporting reproduces the exact baseline.

## Independent system-risk review repair

- Medium — event isolation: cancellation and timeout correctly left every nominal shutdown action and trace row
  unevaluated, but `shutdownFinalState` was calculated after compensation and therefore exposed the rollback
  terminal state as though it were a nominal shutdown result. A consumer could mistake successful compensation,
  or the residual state from incomplete compensation, for shutdown evidence despite `shutdownCompleted=false`.
- Repair: `model.m` now preserves the public field's 1-by-7 numeric shape but returns `NaN` whenever nominal
  shutdown did not complete. `rollbackStateTrace` remains the sole compensation-state record. The MATLAB
  experiment and checks assert that separation, and learner text now explains it explicitly.
- Medium — missing behavioral gate: executed tests covered cancellation/timeout tie precedence only when
  rollback reached safe hold. A refactor could relabel a tied event as timeout when rollback failed while all
  existing executed gates still passed.
- Repair: a new independent-oracle behavioral test runs cancellation, timeout, and their tie with missing
  quiescence, requires nominal shutdown final state to remain unavailable, and pins the tied terminal to
  `cancelled-rollback-incomplete` with `startup-cancelled` and `quiescence-not-confirmed` diagnostics. The retained
  MATLAB checks cover the same compound case; they remain unexecuted without MATLAB.
- Scope: the repair changes only P09-owned model, experiment, checks, tutor text, durable test, and retained
  evidence paths. Public arguments, output names and shapes, manifest lifecycle, P08, shared learner interfaces,
  and the two operator-managed forbidden contract files remain unchanged.
